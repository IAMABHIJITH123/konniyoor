# Konniyoor Legal Compass

Create a modern, professional, and elegant dark-themed website for a law firm / advocate team named "Konniyoor Chambers" (or customize with a team name of choice). The design should look authoritative, trustworthy, and clean, suitable for a legal practice.

Key Sections to Include:

1. Navigation Bar (Navbar):
- Logo/Brand Name on the left ("Konniyoor Chambers").
- Links: Home, About Us, Practice Areas, Our Team, Booking, Contact.
- A prominent "Consult with Us" button on the right.

2. Hero Section:
- A striking headline: "Serving Justice with Integrity & Expertise".
- Subtext highlighting expert legal solutions for civil, criminal, and family litigations.
- Two Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons: "Book Consultation" and "Our Services".

3. About Us Section:
-"Defending Rights. Resolving Disputes. Pursuinmg justice."

4. Practice Areas (Specializations) - Grid Layout:
- Criminal Law
- Civil Lwa
- bail and anyicipatory bail 
- family law 
-Motor Accidents claims Tribunal 
- Consumer Disputes
- Cyber Frauds & Information technology Act
- Insurance Claims
- Mediation & Alternative Dispute Resolution
-Labour & Employment Law
- Other Legal Services 

* Use nice cards with clean icons/accents for each area.

5. Our Team / Panel Section:
- Profiles of senior advocates and panel members with placeholder images, names, designations (e.g., Lead Advocate, Legal Advisor), and brief bios emphasizing their qualifications and experience.

6. Consultation / Booking & Contact Section:
- An interactive appointment booking/inquiry form with fields: First Name, Last Name, Email, Phone, and a dropdown for "Require legal consultation for:" (Practice areas).
- Contact details section showing email, phone numbers, and WhatsApp links.
- Professional footer containing a standard BCI (Bar Council of India) compliance disclaimer, copyright notice, and social media links.

Design & UI Guidelines:
- Color Palette: Deep professional dark background (dark slate/navy/black) with elegant gold, white, or subtle blue accents to give a premium legal feel.
- Fonts: Clean, modern sans-serif typography (like Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).
- Fully responsive layout that looks great on both mobile and desktop screens.
